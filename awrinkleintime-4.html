ARCHIVE FOLDER

Photographs

A collection of photographs documenting families, communities, places and cultural events. Several photographs were removed from public archives after being classified as unnecessary or outdated.

ARCHIVE FOLDER

Music

Catalogue for a collection of traditional songs recorded by community musicians before the recordings were removed from Mzanzi's music library by IT. The catalogue lists song titles, performers, and the communities they belong to.

ARCHIVE FOLDER

History

Records of local events, community histories and historical accounts that became difficult to find through the Mzanzi Connect search system.

ARCHIVE FOLDER

Ancestry

Family trees, oral histories and records showing connections between families and communities. Some records were removed because they could not be verified by the official system.

ARCHIVE FOLDER

Knowledge

Notes and records of indigenous knowledge passed between generations. The archive contains information about plants, practices, local environments and community knowledge.

ARCHIVE FOLDER

Language

Documents containing language records, translations, oral histories and educational material in African languages. Some of these materials became difficult to access through Mzanzi Connect.
